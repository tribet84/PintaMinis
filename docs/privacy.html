1. Who is responsible

PintaMinis is an independent, non-commercial hobby project run by a private individual. The data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is the operator of pintaminis.com.

For any privacy question or request, contact: privacy@pintaminis.com.

2. What is collected

Account data

Email addressYou, when you register; or your Google account

Data you create in the app

  • Which catalogue paints you own, and which are running low or on your shopping list.
  • Custom paint lists you create, and their names.
  • Recipes: names, descriptions, steps, techniques, notes and links you add.
  • Cover photos you upload for a recipe.

Technical data

PintaMinis does not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or profiling of any kind. See the Cookie Policy for exactly what is stored in your browser.

4. What other people can see

Everything you create is private to your account by default.

A recipe becomes visible to other signed-in users only when you explicitly publish it. When you do, the recipe's content, its cover photo and an author name are readable by anyone holding the share link. Published recipes are also grouped on an author page: anyone viewing one of your shared recipes can see the rest of what you currently share. Unsharing a recipe removes it from that page too.

The share link itself carries a public preview: when a link is pasted into a chat or social app, that platform fetches and shows the recipe's name, cover photo and author name — visible to anyone who sees the message, signed in or not. The recipe's content (steps, paints, notes) stays behind sign-in. Unsharing stops the preview within minutes, but copies already rendered inside a chat stay there.

Worth knowing before you publish. The author name is your Google display name if you have one. If you registered with email and password, there is no display name, so the part of your email address before the @ is used instead. If your email address is your real name, that name becomes visible on the published recipe. Set a display name first if you would rather it did not.

Unpublishing a recipe removes the public copy. Anyone who had already saved or copied its content before then is outside our control.

5. Who processes it

PintaMinis does not sell, rent or share your personal data. It is processed on our behalf by the following providers, under their own data processing terms:

ProviderRole
Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC (Firebase) Authentication, database (Cloud Firestore), photo storage (Cloud Storage), hosting, and anti-abuse verification (App Check with reCAPTCHA Enterprise)
Cloudflare, Inc. DNS, content delivery, TLS termination and protection against abuse
GitHub, Inc. Hosts the open-source code only. It holds no personal data and no part of the running service

The app also loads fonts from Google's fonts.gstatic.com, which means your IP address reaches Google when the interface first renders.

The app contains an integration for Google AdMob that is disabled and not compiled into the published build. No advertising data is collected. Should that ever change, this policy will be updated before it does.

6. International transfers

Your data is stored on Google infrastructure in the United States (Cloud Firestore in the nam5 multi-region, Cloud Storage in us-east1). Cloudflare operates a global network and may process connection data outside the EEA.

These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified under it, and otherwise on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long it is kept

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. There is no inactivity purge; nothing is deleted behind your back.

Deleting your account from Settings → Delete account erases, in this order: your inventory, your paint lists, every recipe you published (which also removes it for anyone who linked it), your recipes, your links to other painters' recipes, your uploaded photos, and finally your account itself. This is immediate and cannot be undone.

Provider-level backups and server logs may persist for a short period afterwards under those providers' own retention schedules.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time.

Most of these you can exercise directly in the app, without asking anyone: edit or delete any recipe, list or photo at any time; unpublish a shared recipe to withdraw that consent; delete your entire account from Settings.

For anything else — including a copy of your data in a portable format — write to privacy@pintaminis.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority; in Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.

9. Security

  • All traffic is served over HTTPS, with TLS 1.2 as the minimum version.
  • Database and storage access rules are enforced server-side: your documents are readable and writable only by your own authenticated account.
  • Firebase App Check is enforced on the database and photo storage, so requests that do not come from the genuine app are rejected.
  • Passwords are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication. They are never stored, seen or transmitted by the app itself.

No system is perfectly secure, and this one is run by one person as a hobby. Please do not store anything in it that would be harmful if disclosed.

10. Children

PintaMinis is not directed at children under 14, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe a child has registered, write to the address above and the account will be removed.

11. Changes

This policy may change as the app does. The date at the top always reflects the current version, and material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
