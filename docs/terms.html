1. The service

PintaMinis is a tool for tracking a miniature paint collection: which paints you own, which are running out, what to buy, and how you painted something so you can paint it the same way again.

It is provided free of charge, with no subscription and no payment of any kind. It is an independent hobby project, not a company and not a commercial product. Using it means accepting these terms.

4. Publishing and linking recipes

Recipes are private until you choose to publish one. Publishing makes its content, its photo and an author name readable by any signed-in user holding the link. The link also carries a public preview — the recipe's name, cover photo and author name — shown by chat and social platforms to anyone who sees the link, without an account. See the Privacy Policy for the details and for what that author name is.

  • Other painters bookmark a published recipe rather than copying it. They always see your latest version, and your edits reach them immediately.
  • Unpublishing removes the public copy. It cannot retrieve anything someone already read, screenshotted or wrote down.
  • Deleting your account unpublishes everything you shared.
